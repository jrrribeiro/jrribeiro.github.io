Opening note

Between listening and evidence

Recife, Brazil

A recording preserves more than a sound: it holds traces of presence, rhythm, and change that may otherwise pass unnoticed in the field.

My work begins where birds, soundscapes, and data meet. The aim is to frame useful ecological questions, organize evidence carefully, and build tools that make research clearer and more reproducible.

01 · Research

Intersecting lines of inquiry

My research uses sound and ecological data to investigate questions about birds, populations, communities, and conservation.

01

Passive acoustic monitoring

Using sound recordings as a non-invasive way to infer acoustic occurrence and patterns of vocal activity through time.

02

Community and population ecology

Questions about composition, abundance, dynamics, and how birds respond to environmental variation.

03

Conservation

Connecting ecological evidence with decisions that support the conservation of birds and their habitats.

04

Bird bioacoustics

Sound as both a subject of study and a source of information for investigating ecological patterns.
